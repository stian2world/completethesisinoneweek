How to play

Hand gestures

Keep your hands at chest height so the camera can see them.

Squeeze
One hand. Open palm on the cloud, then clench it into a fist
Grab
One hand. Clench and keep holding (or move the cloud)
Pull
Two fists on the cloud, pull apart
Slam
One hand. Grab it, lift it, swing down hard

Without a camera

Squeeze
Click the cloud
Grab
Press and hold still
Pull
Drag from the edge of the cloud outward
Slam
Drag from the middle and fling it

Demo keys: 1 Squeeze · 2 Grab · 3 Pull · 4 Slam · R Reset
